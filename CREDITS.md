# Credits and licences

Ashfall's own code, data, story text and art are released under the **MIT licence** (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). This
page lists every third-party source that Ashfall **bundles** (ships inside its jars or the `.mrpack`) or **derives
from**, and every mod the pack **references** without redistributing it.

Audit date: 2026-10-26 (Ashfall 1.0-rc). Method:
- grepped every resource folder, tool and source header for licence, attribution, author and "based on" notes;
- checked every shipped texture, model and sound against its generator or source;
- checked the jar-in-jar `include` lines in the Gradle builds;
- read the mod licences from each Modrinth project page.

No other third-party code is copied into Ashfall. In particular, **no code or assets from PSWG (Galaxies) or any
other LGPL project were borrowed**: the grep found no such source, and the blasters, bolts, blades and their art are
Ashfall's own. The Optic scope (2026-10) was designed after studying how PSWG's blasters zoom (its
`BlasterZoomHandler`, which drives LibZoomer with a smooth transition, a zoom-divided mouse modifier and a
spyglass-style overlay, and its first-person item hiding while the overlay is up). Only the behaviour was taken: the
code in `ScopeMath`, `ScopeClient`, `ScopeOverlay` and the scope mixins is written from scratch, and the overlay
texture is Ashfall's own (`tools/textures/hud_scope.py`), not PSWG's. Other mods are reached only through their public APIs, with compile-only dependencies. That includes
Lost Cities, Open Parties and Claims, Zombie Break & Build and Contagion. Torch burnout (salvage `TorchBurnout`, 10.3.0)
is Ashfall's own code and art, written after Realistic Torches left the pack; none of that mod's code or assets was used.

### Original to Ashfall (no third-party source)
- **Item, block, entity and HUD textures.** Every PNG under `modules/*/src/main/resources/assets/ashfall/textures/`
  is drawn by code in `tools/textures/` and `tools/models/` (`gen_textures.py`, `gen_block_textures.py`,
  `gen_entity_textures.py`, `hud_thirst.py`, `hud_infection.py`, `hud_scope.py` for the blaster scope overlay).
- **3D models.** The blaster, blade, bench and block models come from `tools/models/*.py`.
- **Vanilla assets used at runtime, not copied.** The special infected skins are overlays drawn over the player's own
  vanilla zombie texture. Bolt and impact particles reuse vanilla particle sprites by id. Neither is in our jars.
- **Landmark buildings** (safehouse, police station, hospital, Kiln & Co. Salvage, four substations, pharmacy,
  Wreck hull, crash sites) are generated by `tools/landmarks/` and `tools/sites/`.
- **Story text:** the 30 Relay Logs, the site documents, the Survivor's Notes and the ending pages.

### Retired art pipeline (in the repository, not shipped)
`tools/legacy_art/` holds the scripts of an earlier render pipeline that turned CC0 3D models into item icons. Its
source list (`tools/legacy_art/kenney/art/sources.json`) names Kenney's Blaster Kit, Survival Kit and Food Kit, Poly
Pizza models by Dipper98, iPoly3D, Quaternius, MilkAndBanana, hat_my_guy and Isa Lousberg, and OpenGameArt's "duct
tape" by darkrose, all CC0. None of its output is in the current jars: those icons were replaced by the code-drawn
textures above. The source models themselves were never committed.
